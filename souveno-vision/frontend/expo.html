Modelled hall & best stalls

        
Until the organiser's plan arrives, this is a modelled layout of the venue's hall: entrance on the south wall, main aisle north, 3 m shell stalls numbered per row from the west. Darker = better. Scoring: traffic 30 · main/cross aisle 20 · corner 20 · anchor 10 · amenities 10 · penalties for back wall, dead-ends, noisy zones, pillars.
